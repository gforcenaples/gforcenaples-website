What every visit includes

  • Visual walkthrough of accessible interior and exterior areas
  • Look for visible water intrusion, leaks, pests and damage
  • Check doors, windows and thermostat where accessible
  • Observe landscaping, irrigation and storm-related concerns
  • Collect accessible mail and bring delivered packages inside
  • Photo updates and notice of concerns requiring attention

Optional add-ons

  • Visual pool-water and equipment-area check, including apparent pump operation — additional fee
  • Arrival preparation, shopping and restocking — separately quoted concierge service
  • Vendor access and project coordination — separately quoted
  • Additional visits or special requests — quoted before scheduling

Pricing & service guidelines

Prices shown are starting monthly subscription rates. Your quote depends on property type and size, access, number of buildings, pool/pump checks, location, requested tasks and visit complexity. Weekly and biweekly plans are based on 52 and 26 visits per year respectively, billed monthly. Home Watch is a visual observation service, not a home inspection, guarantee against loss, emergency response or licensed pool/HVAC service. George will report concerns and coordinate qualified specialists when requested; contractor charges are separate. Visits and mail handling are subject to safe access and agreed instructions.
