TAILORED PROPERTY OVERSIGHT

What we check during a Home Watch visit

Each property receives an agreed, customized checklist. Inspections are non-invasive visual observations, with photo documentation and prompt reporting of visible concerns.

Interior, plumbing and moisture

  • Look for visible leaks under accessible sinks, around toilets, water heaters and appliances
  • Observe ceilings, walls, baseboards and accessible areas for water stains, dampness or unusual odors
  • Run faucets and flush toilets when authorized by the homeowner
  • Look for signs of insects, rodents or other pest activity

HVAC, humidity and appliances

  • Record indoor temperature and humidity; verify thermostat settings
  • Observe AC operation indicators and visible condensate or water concerns
  • Visually check accessible AC filter condition
  • Observe refrigerator, freezer and other designated appliances for obvious issues

Security and general condition

  • Check accessible doors, windows and garage closure
  • Observe alarm status if access and authorization are provided
  • Note unusual noises, odors, visible damage or signs of forced entry
  • Provide visit photos and a written summary; escalate concerns according to homeowner instructions

Exterior, pool and outdoor areas — where applicable

  • Ground-level visual check of accessible exterior walls, roofline, windows and doors
  • Observe lanai, pool cage, screens, pool/spa appearance and visible equipment indicators
  • Look for storm debris, drainage issues, landscape concerns and visible irrigation leaks
  • Check accessible driveway, garage, outdoor kitchen and detached areas identified in the service plan

Home Watch is a visual property-observation service, not a licensed home inspection, HVAC diagnostic service, mold assessment, pool maintenance service or guarantee against loss. Specific checklist items depend on safe access, homeowner authorization and property features.

CUSTOM PROPERTY PRICING

Every property is different

Your quote reflects the actual time and attention your property requires. A compact condo with one AC system is different from a large single-family home with multiple systems and outdoor amenities.

Factors we consider

  • Property type: condo, villa, townhome, single-family home or luxury estate
  • Approximate size: under 1,500; 1,500–2,500; 2,500–4,000; 4,000–6,000; or over 6,000 square feet
  • Complexity: bathrooms, kitchens, AC systems, appliances, garages and separate structures
  • Outdoor features: pool, spa, lanai, outdoor kitchen, landscaping and irrigation
  • Service needs: visit frequency, reporting, maintenance, vendor access and travel

We can also create a custom schedule and checklist for unique homes or homeowner requirements.
