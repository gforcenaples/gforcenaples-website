Project coordination support

For larger improvements and multi-trade projects, G-Force can help you connect with a network of trusted service providers, compare estimates, coordinate work, and follow up on progress. We help monitor visible work against agreed plans, communicate concerns, and share photo updates. Licensed contractors remain responsible for regulated trade work, permits, and technical supervision; schedules and outcomes depend on the providers and scope.

  • Discuss scope, priorities, timing and budget goals
  • Help collect and organize vendor estimates
  • Coordinate schedules and access with participating trades
  • Communicate project updates and milestones
  • Document visible progress and follow-up items
  • Track punch-list items and help arrange final walkthroughs
  • Provide a single point of contact for agreed coordination tasks
  • Help identify and connect with trusted providers suited to the project
  • Share photo updates and flag visible quality concerns for contractor follow-up
  • Follow up on milestones and timing, and communicate scheduling changes

Tailored to your project

Every project is different. We provide a written coordination proposal based on complexity, timeline, site visits and communication needs. Contractor work is quoted separately.
