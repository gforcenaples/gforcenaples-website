Examples of small handyman tasks

Not included in the subscription

Pricing & service guidelines

Starting rates vary with property size, condition, access, travel and task complexity. Essential is billed monthly with one scheduled quarterly visit; Plus is billed monthly with one scheduled monthly visit. Included handyman time is part of the total visit allowance, not additional time. Unused time does not roll over. Work is limited to small, safe, permitted tasks and is subject to available time; larger work is quoted separately. Materials and any third-party contractor charges are extra. Exact scope and schedule are confirmed in the written quote.
